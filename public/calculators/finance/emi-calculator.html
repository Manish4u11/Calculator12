EMI Calculator - Calculate Your Loan EMI Online Free

An EMI Calculator (Equated Monthly Installment Calculator) is an essential financial tool that helps you calculate your monthly loan repayment amount with precision. Whether you're planning to take a home loan, car loan, personal loan, or business loan, our free online EMI calculator provides instant, accurate results to help you plan your finances effectively.

Understanding your EMI before taking a loan is crucial for financial planning. It helps you assess your repayment capacity, compare different loan offers, and make informed borrowing decisions.

What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment - a fixed amount you pay to the lender every month until your loan is fully repaid.

How to Use Our EMI Calculator?

Enter loan amount, interest rate, and tenure to calculate your monthly EMI instantly.

EMI Calculation Formula

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n / [(1 + r)^n - 1]

Where:
P = Principal Loan Amount
r = Monthly Interest Rate
n = Loan Tenure in Months
