Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about Calculator12.

General Questions

What is Calculator12?

Calculator12 is a free online platform offering 340+ calculators across 27+ categories including finance, health, math, science, and lifestyle. Our mission is to make calculations accessible to everyone, everywhere, in 100+ languages.

Is Calculator12 really free?

Yes, 100% free forever. There are no registration fees, no premium tiers, no hidden costs, and no usage limits. We're funded through ethical advertising that never compromises the user experience.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is required. You can use all calculators anonymously without providing any personal information. Your privacy is our priority.

In how many languages is Calculator12 available?

Calculator12 is currently available in 13 languages including English, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Russian, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, and Indonesian. We're continuously adding more languages based on user demand.

Can I use Calculator12 on my mobile phone?

Absolutely! Our website is fully responsive and works perfectly on smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktop computers. All calculators are optimized for touch interfaces and smaller screens.

How many calculators do you offer?

We currently offer 340+ calculators across 27+ categories, and we're adding new ones every month based on user feedback and emerging needs. Our goal is to reach 500+ calculators by 2027.

Accuracy & Reliability

How accurate are your calculators?

All our calculators use industry-standard mathematical and financial formulas that are reviewed by domain experts. We conduct quarterly audits to maintain the highest accuracy standards. Results are accurate to multiple decimal places, making them suitable for both personal and professional use.

Can I trust the results for professional use?

Our calculators are suitable for personal, educational, and preliminary professional use. However, for critical financial, legal, medical, or tax decisions, we always recommend consulting qualified professionals. Our tools are designed to assist, not replace, expert advice.

What formulas do you use?

We use internationally recognized formulas from authoritative sources like the World Health Organization (WHO) for health calculators, standard financial formulas for finance calculators, and established mathematical principles for math calculators. Each calculator page shows the exact formula used.

Do you show step-by-step calculations?

Yes! Most of our calculators provide detailed step-by-step breakdowns showing exactly how the result was calculated. This helps users understand the process and verify the accuracy of the results.

What should I do if I find an error?

If you believe a calculator is giving incorrect results, please use the feedback button on that calculator page or email us at bugs@calculator12.com. Include the calculator name, your inputs, the result you received, and the result you expected. We investigate all reports within 48 hours.

Privacy & Security

Do you collect my calculation data?

No. All calculations happen entirely in your browser. We never receive, store, or transmit your calculation inputs or results to our servers. Your data stays on your device, ensuring complete privacy.

Do you use cookies?

We use minimal cookies only for essential functionality like remembering your language preference, theme choice (light/dark mode), and currency selection. We do NOT use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or social media cookies. Read our Cookie Policy for full details.

Is my personal information safe?

We don't collect personal information in the first place. You can use all our calculators without providing your name, email, phone number, or any other personal details. For more details, please read our Privacy Policy.

Do you sell user data?

Absolutely not. We don't collect user data, so there's nothing to sell. Our business model is based on ethical advertising, not data monetization. Your privacy is non-negotiable.

Are your calculators secure?

Yes. Our website uses HTTPS encryption to protect data in transit. Since calculations happen in your browser, there's no server-side processing of your sensitive data. We also regularly update our security practices to protect against emerging threats.

Support & Feedback

How can I contact support?

You can reach our support team at support@calculator12.com. We respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours during business days (Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM UTC). For urgent issues, please mark your email subject with "[URGENT]".

Absolutely! We love new ideas. Email us at ideas@calculator12.com with a description of the calculator you'd like to see, the formula or logic it should use, and why it would be useful. We review all suggestions monthly and prioritize based on user demand.

Use the feedback button on any calculator page, or email bugs@calculator12.com with details about the issue. Include the calculator name, steps to reproduce the problem, expected vs. actual result, and your browser/device information.

Technical Questions

Do you offer an API?

We're currently exploring API options for enterprise users. If you're interested in API access for your business or application, please email partnerships@calculator12.com with details about your use case and expected volume.

What technologies do you use?

Our frontend is built with vanilla JavaScript, HTML5, and CSS3 for maximum performance. We use Node.js for backend services, PostgreSQL for databases, and deploy on cloud infrastructure. We're always evaluating new technologies that could improve our platform.

Why are some calculators faster than others?

Simple calculators like percentage or BMI provide instant results because they require minimal computation. Complex calculators like currency converters or those with detailed amortization schedules may take a moment longer due to real-time data fetching or complex calculations.

Do calculators work offline?

Most of our calculators require an internet connection to load. However, once loaded, many calculators can perform calculations offline since the logic runs in your browser. Currency converters and other real-time tools require an active connection for live data.

Which browsers do you support?

We support all modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, and Opera. We recommend using the latest version of your preferred browser for the best experience. Internet Explorer is not supported.
