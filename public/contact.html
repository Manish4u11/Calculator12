What to Include in Your Message

To help us respond as quickly and accurately as possible, please include the following details in your message. The more information you provide, the faster we can help you:

  • For Bug Reports: Name of the calculator, step-by-step instructions to reproduce the issue, expected result vs. actual result, your browser name and version, your device type (desktop/mobile/tablet), and screenshots if possible.
  • For Feature Requests: Clear description of the calculator or feature you need, the specific use case or problem it would solve, any formulas or logic you'd like us to follow, and similar tools you've seen elsewhere (optional).
  • For General Questions: Your question written in as much detail as possible, the calculator you were using when the question arose, and your preferred language for our response.
  • For Partnership Inquiries: Your organization name and website, a brief summary of your proposal, how the partnership would benefit Calculator12 users, and your contact information including phone number if preferred.

Our Response Time

Support Hours

Our support team is available Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM (UTC). We strive to respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours during business days. For urgent issues, please mark your email subject line with "[URGENT]" and we'll prioritize your request.

Please note that messages received on weekends or public holidays will be addressed on the next business day. We appreciate your patience and understanding. Rest assured, no message goes unanswered — we respond to every single inquiry we receive.

Our Support Philosophy

At Calculator12, we believe that excellent support is not just about solving problems — it's about building relationships. Every user who takes the time to contact us is helping us improve, and we treat that trust with the utmost respect.

We never use automated chatbots to handle support requests. When you email us, a real person reads your message, understands your situation, and crafts a personalized response. We believe this human touch is essential, especially when dealing with calculations that might affect important financial, health, or educational decisions.

We also believe in transparency. If we don't know the answer to your question, we'll tell you honestly and work to find the answer. If a feature you requested isn't on our roadmap, we'll explain why and suggest alternatives. Our goal is to be honest, helpful, and human in every interaction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer API access for developers?

Currently, we don't offer a public API, but we're actively exploring this option for enterprise users. If you're interested in API access for your business or application, please email partnerships@calculator12.com with details about your use case and expected volume.

Can I advertise on Calculator12?

We accept ethical advertising partnerships that align with our values of accuracy, accessibility, and user privacy. We do not accept ads for gambling, adult content, or misleading financial products. Please contact partnerships@calculator12.com with your proposal.

How can I report a calculation error?

If you believe a calculator is giving incorrect results, please email bugs@calculator12.com with the calculator name, the inputs you used, the result you received, and the result you expected. We investigate all reported errors within 48 hours and notify you of our findings.

Do you offer calculators in my language?

Calculator12 is currently available in 13 languages including English, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Russian, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, and Indonesian. If you'd like to see your language added, please email us at support@calculator12.com.

Can I use Calculator12 for commercial purposes?

Our calculators are free for personal and educational use. For commercial use, embedding on other websites, or white-label solutions, please contact partnerships@calculator12.com to discuss licensing options that work for your business.

How do I suggest a new calculator?

We love new ideas! Email ideas@calculator12.com with a description of the calculator you'd like to see, the formula or logic it should use, and why it would be useful. We review all suggestions monthly and prioritize based on user demand.
