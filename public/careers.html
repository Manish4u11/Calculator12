Why Work at Calculator12?

At Calculator12, we're on a mission to make calculations accessible to everyone. We're a remote-first, diverse team of passionate individuals who believe in the power of free tools. If you're excited about building products that impact millions of lives daily, we'd love to have you on board.

We're not just building calculators — we're building trust. Every day, millions of people rely on our tools to make important financial, health, and educational decisions. That's a responsibility we take seriously, and it's what drives our team to deliver excellence in everything we do. Join us, and your work will genuinely matter.

Our Culture & Values

Calculator12 is built on a foundation of transparency, collaboration, and continuous learning. We believe the best ideas come from diverse perspectives, which is why our team spans 15 countries across 6 continents. We celebrate different viewpoints, encourage respectful debate, and empower every team member to take ownership of their work.

We operate with a "users first" mindset. Every feature we build, every bug we fix, and every decision we make is guided by one question: "Does this help our users?" This clarity of purpose keeps us aligned and motivated, even when tackling complex challenges. We're a team that works hard, laughs often, and supports each other through both successes and setbacks.

Life as a Remote Team

Being remote-first isn't just a policy at Calculator12 — it's part of our identity. We've built our culture around asynchronous communication, documented processes, and trust. You'll have the flexibility to work when you're most productive, whether that's early morning, late night, or somewhere in between.

We use tools like Slack for daily communication, Notion for documentation, Zoom for video calls, and GitHub for collaboration. Weekly team syncs keep everyone aligned, while monthly virtual socials and annual in-person retreats help us build genuine relationships. You'll never feel isolated — you'll feel connected to a global team that has your back.

How to Apply

Ready to join us? We'd love to hear from you. Here's how to get started:

  • Check Open Positions: Visit our careers page or LinkedIn profile to see current openings that match your skills.
  • Prepare Your Application: Update your resume and write a brief cover letter explaining why you're excited about Calculator12 and what you'd bring to the team.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you sponsor work visas?

Currently, we're a fully remote company and don't sponsor visas. However, we hire globally as independent contractors or through Employer of Record services in select countries. If you're in a country where we don't have legal entity coverage, we can often work through platforms like Deel or Remote.com.

What's the interview process like?

Our process includes: initial application review, 30-minute screening call, practical skills assessment, team interview, and final culture fit discussion. The entire process takes 2-3 weeks, and we provide feedback at every stage.

Do you offer internships?

Yes! We offer paid internships for students and recent graduates in engineering, design, content, and data roles. Internships are typically 3-6 months and can lead to full-time positions. Check our careers page for current openings.

What are the working hours?

We're flexible! While we have core overlap hours (10 AM - 2 PM UTC) for team meetings, you're free to structure the rest of your day as you see fit. We care about results, not hours logged.

Can I work part-time?

We're open to part-time arrangements for certain roles, especially for experienced professionals. Mention your preference in your application, and we'll discuss what's possible during the interview process.

What tech stack do you use?

Our frontend is built with vanilla JavaScript, HTML5, and CSS3 for maximum performance. We use Node.js for backend services, PostgreSQL for databases, and deploy on cloud infrastructure. We're always evaluating new technologies that could improve our platform.
