1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy should be read in conjunction with our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, tablet, or other device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide a better user experience, and give website owners useful information about how their sites are being used.

Cookies themselves do not contain or collect any personal information that can identify you. However, when combined with other data, they can help us understand how you interact with our Site. Cookies are stored in your web browser and can be accessed by the website that created them on subsequent visits.

Good News

Calculator12 uses minimal cookies. We prioritize your privacy and only use cookies that are essential for the Site to function properly or that significantly enhance your experience.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

We use different types of cookies for different purposes. Here's a detailed breakdown of each type:

Essential Cookies

These cookies are strictly necessary for the Site to function properly. Without them, you cannot use core features of our Site.

  • Session management
  • Security features
  • Load balancing

Preference Cookies

These cookies remember your choices and settings to provide a personalized experience each time you visit.

  • Language selection
  • Theme (light/dark mode)
  • Currency preference

Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our Site by collecting and reporting information anonymously.

  • Page views and traffic
  • Popular calculators
  • User journey tracking

Advertising Cookies

We do NOT use advertising cookies. We believe in a clean, non-intrusive experience for our users.

  • No targeted ads
  • No tracking pixels
  • No ad networks

5. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following specific purposes:

  • To Remember Your Preferences: When you select a language, theme, or currency, we store this in a cookie so you don't have to reselect it every time you visit.
  • To Maintain Your Session: Essential cookies help us keep track of your session as you navigate between different pages of our Site.
  • To Improve Performance: Analytics cookies help us understand which calculators are most popular, which pages have issues, and how we can improve the overall user experience.
  • To Ensure Security: Some cookies help us protect against cross-site request forgery (CSRF) attacks and other security threats.
  • To Load Balance: Technical cookies help us distribute traffic across our servers to ensure fast, reliable performance.

We do NOT use cookies to: track your browsing history across other websites, build a profile of your interests for advertising purposes, sell your data to third parties, or identify you personally.

7. Managing Your Cookies

You have full control over cookies. Here are several ways you can manage them:

Browser Settings

Most browsers allow you to view, manage, delete, and block cookies. Check your browser's help menu for instructions.

Block All Cookies

You can set your browser to block all cookies, but this may affect the functionality of our Site and many other websites.

Delete Existing Cookies

You can delete cookies that have already been set. However, you may have to manually adjust some preferences every time you visit.

Private Browsing

Use private or incognito mode to browse without storing cookies. Cookies are deleted when you close the browser.

Browser-Specific Instructions

For detailed instructions on managing cookies in your specific browser, visit: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge.

8. Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you choose to disable cookies, please be aware that this may affect your experience on our Site. Here's what might happen:

  • Language Won't Be Remembered: You'll need to select your preferred language every time you visit.
  • Theme Reset: The Site will default to light mode on every visit, even if you prefer dark mode.
  • Currency Reset: Your preferred currency won't be saved between sessions.
  • Reduced Functionality: Some features may not work properly without essential cookies.
  • Slower Experience: Without session cookies, you may experience slower page loads.

We recommend keeping essential and preference cookies enabled for the best experience. You can safely disable analytics cookies without affecting core functionality.

9. Children's Privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information through cookies, please contact us immediately.

We encourage parents and guardians to monitor their children's online activities and consider using parental control tools to manage cookie usage.
