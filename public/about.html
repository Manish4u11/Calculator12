About Calculator12

Empowering millions with free, accurate, and accessible calculation tools since 2020.

Our Story

Calculator12 was founded in 2020 with a simple yet powerful mission: to make mathematical and financial calculations accessible to everyone, everywhere. What started as a small collection of 10 calculators has grown into a comprehensive platform featuring 340+ tools used by millions of people across 100+ countries.

Our founder, a former financial analyst, experienced firsthand how confusing calculations could be when planning loans, investments, and taxes. Frustrated by the lack of simple, free tools, they decided to build Calculator12 — a platform where anyone could get instant, accurate results without registration fees or hidden costs.

Today, Calculator12 serves students, professionals, entrepreneurs, and everyday users who need quick, reliable answers. From calculating monthly EMI payments to tracking BMI, from converting units to planning investments, our platform has become a trusted companion for millions worldwide.

Our Mission

To democratize access to calculation tools by providing 100% free, accurate, and user-friendly calculators that help people make informed decisions in finance, health, education, and daily life. We believe that everyone deserves access to reliable computational tools, regardless of their background or location.

We envision a world where complex calculations are no longer a barrier to progress. Whether you're a student in rural India learning percentages, a small business owner in Brazil calculating taxes, or a parent in Japan planning their child's education fund — Calculator12 is here to help, in your language, on your device, at no cost.
