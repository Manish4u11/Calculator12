Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. We never collect or sell your personal data.

Last Updated: January 1, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Calculator12 ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website calculator12.com (the "Site").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using our Site, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by all the terms outlined in this policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Site.

Information We Collect

We believe in collecting only the minimum information necessary to provide you with the best possible experience. Here's a detailed breakdown of what we collect and why:

Type of Information Purpose Stored
Browser Type & Version Optimize user experience No (processed only)
Device Information Ensure mobile compatibility No (processed only)
Language Preference Remember your language choice Yes (in your browser only)
Theme Preference Remember light/dark mode Yes (in your browser only)
Calculation Inputs Perform calculations Never
Calculation Results Display results Never

Key Point

All calculations happen entirely in your browser. Your calculation inputs and results are never sent to our servers, stored in our databases, or shared with any third party.

How We Use Information

The limited information we collect is used solely to improve your experience on our Site. Specifically, we use this information to:

  • Improve Performance: Understand which calculators are most popular so we can optimize their performance and add similar tools.
  • Fix Bugs: Identify and resolve technical issues to ensure smooth operation across all devices and browsers.
  • Enhance User Experience: Remember your preferences (language, theme, currency) so you don't have to set them every time you visit.
  • Develop New Features: Use anonymous usage patterns to guide our development roadmap and prioritize new calculator categories.
  • Ensure Security: Monitor for suspicious activity and protect our Site from malicious attacks.

We never use your information for targeted advertising, sell it to third parties, or share it with data brokers. Your privacy is our top priority.

Cookies and Local Storage

We use minimal cookies and browser local storage to enhance your experience. Here's exactly what we store and why:

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the Site to function properly. They enable basic functions like page navigation and access to secure areas. The Site cannot function properly without these cookies.

Preference Cookies

These cookies enable the Site to remember information that changes the way the Site behaves or looks, like your preferred language, theme (light/dark mode), and currency selection.

Analytics Cookies (Optional)

We may use anonymous analytics cookies to understand how visitors interact with our Site. These cookies collect information in an anonymous form, including the number of visitors, where visitors come from, and the pages they visit. You can opt out of these cookies at any time.

We do NOT use: advertising cookies, tracking cookies, social media cookies, or third-party marketing cookies. You can control cookies through your browser settings at any time.
